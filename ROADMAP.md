# Ideas to hand to Claude

Each of these is written to paste straight into a new issue. Keep "@claude" in the body so it starts.

## Shuffle feel for the click

> @claude Add a shuffle/swing option to the click options so the subdivision clicks can swing (straight, light swing, full triplet shuffle). Add a "Shuffle pump" exercise to the Time group: eighth-note shuffle on one note, with the same stage progression idea as the quarter-note pump. Most of what I play in the band is shuffle-based rock.

## Timing check with the mic

> @claude Add an optional timing check to the player. With mic permission, detect my note onsets while I play along and show, after each run, whether I'm rushing or dragging on average (in milliseconds) and how consistent I am. Start with the quarter-note pump and staccato sixteenths. Calibrate for output latency with a tap test.

## Tuner

> @claude Add a simple bass tuner (E A D G, plus drop D) reachable from the Today header. Big note name and a needle readable from a music stand.

## Install as an app, works offline

> @claude Make the app installable and work offline: service worker that caches the app shell and fonts. Check that the home-screen icon and status bar look right on iPhone.

## More right-hand drills

> @claude Add exercises: eighth-note pump (rock eighths, palm-muted option), raking across strings, and ghost notes in sixteenth grooves. Put them in the dexterity rotation.

## Song section looping

> @claude For songs, let me add sections (intro, verse, chorus, solo) each with its own practice tempo, and pick which section to work on in the player.

## Click sounds

> @claude Add a choice of click sound in Settings: beep (current), woodblock, hi-hat, cowbell. Synthesize them in Web Audio, no audio files.

## Practice reminders

> @claude Show a streak on the Today tab (days in a row with at least one logged run) and a gentle line when I haven't practiced for 2+ days.
